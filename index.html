Who Are We?

Prakash M Oswal & Co. is a premier, Peer-Reviewed Chartered Accountancy firm based in Pune. We are a full-service practice committed to delivering holistic financial solutions to startups, SMEs, and established corporates across diverse industries.

With a robust team of 80+ professionals and decades of legacy, our capabilities encompass statutory and internal audits, complex taxation and compliance, precise accounting, dynamic transaction advisory (M&A, due diligence, valuation), and strategic Virtual CFO services. We pride ourselves on uncompromised integrity, rigorous discipline, and a modern, tech-savvy approach to navigating today's complex regulatory landscapes.

Strategic + Practical

We combine board-level strategic perspective with hands-on execution. Whether it is ensuring a flawless statutory audit, structuring a complex M&A transaction, or optimizing daily cash flows, our high-involvement model ensures results.

Modern + Tech-Savvy

Fluent across mainstream accounting, ERP, and reporting tools. We support seamless remote and hybrid engagements, enabling robust pan-India and cross-border delivery tailored to your specific business scale.
